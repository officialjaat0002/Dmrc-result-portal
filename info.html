Info regards Delhi Metro Recruitment Exam.

Important Notice

This is an official information page and is the official website of Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC).

1. Age Related Note
  • Candidate's Who are UNDER-AGED as on the cut-off date (09-09-2026). Are requested to read below instructions carefully
  • For the Candidates above 18 yr can join after document verfication and valid Engineering degree verification.
  • As per DMRC recruitment rules, minimum age requirement for Asst. CRA is 18 years.
  • Candidate has been provisionally allowed to appear and is provisionally selected subject to attaining the minimum age before final joining / appointment.
  • Candidate needs to complete BCA in AI & ML alongside the recruitment process.
  • Candidate needs to submit college admission details and provisional certificate of BCA enrolled in 2026. on the day of final Document verification day.
2. Further Details
Further Steps
Document Verification followed by Medical Examination
Status
Provisionally Selected (Subject to fulfillment of all eligibility conditions)
Additional Condition
Candidate must complete BCA in AI & ML alongside the recruitment process.
Interview / Final Selection
Will be subject to successful document verification and medical fitness.
3. Important Instructions / Eligibility Conditions
  • All original documents (educational certificates, age proof, category certificate if applicable, identity proof, etc.) must be produced in original along with self-attested photocopies at the time of Document Verification.
  • Failure to produce required documents or discrepancy in information will lead to cancellation of candidature.
  • Medical Examination will be conducted as per applicable standards. Candidate must be medically fit.
  • The selection is purely provisional and does not confer any right to appointment.
  • Any false information or suppression of facts will result in cancellation of candidature and may lead to appropriate action.
  • Candidate is advised to regularly check the official DMRC website for further updates regarding Document Verification schedule, venue, and medical examination dates.
  • No TA/DA will be paid for appearing in Document Verification or Medical Examination.
  • The decision of DMRC regarding eligibility, selection, and appointment shall be final and binding.
